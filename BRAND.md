# Brand & Design Rules

## Visual style

The visual style should feel:

- Premium
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Professional
- Modern

Inspired by Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Notion, Raycast, and Framer.

- Whitespace is intentional — never fill space just to fill it.
- Typography carries the design — hierarchy and rhythm come from type, not decoration.
- Avoid decorative effects (gradients, shadows, animation) unless they improve usability or comprehension.

## Accessibility

All UI must meet WCAG AA:

- Semantic HTML
- Full keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Proper labels on all interactive elements
- Accessible forms (error messaging, associated labels, valid states)
