# Brand Experience Principles

## Core principles

**Consistency** — the same quality regardless of division, product, platform, device, or channel. **Simplicity** — complex processes should feel effortless. **Reliability** — meeting deadlines, transparent communication, stable products, responsive support. **Human-centered** — technology and process adapt to people, not the reverse. **Excellence** — craftsmanship visible in every detail, from design to documentation to customer service.

## Customer journey stages

1. **Discovery** — website, search, referral, events. Customer should feel curiosity, confidence, professionalism.
2. **Exploration** — services, portfolio, case studies, pricing. Should feel trust, clarity, credibility.
3. **Engagement** — consultation, booking, proposal. Should feel welcomed, heard, supported.
4. **Delivery** — the actual product/service/booking. Should demonstrate professionalism, transparency, efficiency, quality.
5. **Support** — post-delivery relationship. Customer should feel valued beyond the initial transaction.
6. **Advocacy** — the goal state: repeat customer, referral partner, long-term collaborator.

## Digital experience

Every digital product should share: clean layouts, fast performance, clear navigation, accessible interfaces, consistent typography, predictable interactions, professional microcopy, thoughtful (not decorative) animation.

## Experience by division

- **Unda Studio** — creative, collaborative, inspiring, detail-oriented.
- **Unda Technologies** — reliable, technical, structured, efficient.
- **Unda Travel** — friendly, reassuring, organized, stress-free.

Despite the differences, every interaction should still read as unmistakably Unda.

## Touchpoints this applies to

Digital (websites, products, docs, emails), physical (cards, signage, merchandise), human (meetings, support, consultations), marketing (social, ads, case studies). See `../design-system/rules/` for the concrete, enforceable versions of these principles.
