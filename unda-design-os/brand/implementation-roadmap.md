# Brand Implementation Roadmap

## Philosophy

Build the foundation first, then scale with confidence — the same underlying instinct as this monorepo's "build first, abstract second" (with `unda-design-os` itself being the documented exception, see `../../DECISIONS.md` ADR-009).

## Phases (adapted to this project's actual state)

1. **Foundation** — this Design OS itself: brand strategy, foundations, tokens, component/pattern docs. *(In progress.)*
2. **Digital presence** — `apps/web` gets real content built from this OS's foundations/tokens. *(Not started — this is `ROADMAP.md` phase 8 at the monorepo root.)*
3. **Product integration** — `packages/ui`/`packages/design-system` extracted from real `apps/web` components once patterns repeat. *(Not started — deliberately deferred per ADR-002.)*
4. **Brand operations** — email signatures, presentation templates, business cards, internal docs. *(Not started — no real operational need yet at this company size.)*
5. **Marketing & communications** — social templates, content system, case studies. *(Not started.)*
6. **Physical brand** — signage, merchandise, event branding. *(Not applicable yet.)*
7. **Ecosystem expansion** — Studio/Technologies/Travel get real content once requested (see monorepo `CLAUDE.md` — currently explicitly out of scope).

## What NOT to do with this roadmap

Don't treat "the roadmap says Phase 4 is Brand Operations" as permission to go build business card templates for a five-person company with one placeholder website. This roadmap describes an eventual sequence for a company that has scaled — check the monorepo's actual `ROADMAP.md` for what's real *right now*.
