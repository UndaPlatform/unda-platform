# Measuring Brand Success

## Principle

What gets measured can be improved — but measurement should wait until there's something real to measure. Right now, most of the metrics below have no data behind them because there's no live product beyond a "coming soon" page. Don't fabricate a dashboard for numbers that don't exist yet.

## Six dimensions (for later)

Brand recognition, brand consistency, customer experience, product experience, employee adoption, business impact. Concrete indicators (NPS, CSAT, component reuse rate, accessibility compliance, etc.) are listed in the source Brand Foundation Manual — they become relevant once `apps/web` and at least one division site have real traffic and usage.

## Continuous improvement cycle

Observe → Measure → Analyze → Prioritize → Improve → Validate → Document → Repeat. Applies once there's something running in production to observe.

## What to actually do right now

- Log architectural/brand decisions as ADRs in the monorepo root `DECISIONS.md` — that's the improvement loop that's actually active today.
- Revisit this file once `apps/web` ships real content and has real visitors.
