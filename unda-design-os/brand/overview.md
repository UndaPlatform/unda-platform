# Company Overview

## What Unda Solutions is

Unda Solutions is a multidisciplinary technology and business solutions company operating across three (soon four, with Docs) divisions: **Unda Studio** (creative services), **Unda Technologies** (software engineering, AI, automation), and **Unda Travel** (travel management). Rather than functioning as a pure service provider, the company is building an ecosystem of products, platforms, and services intended to create long-term value across Africa and beyond.

## Mission

> To create intelligent digital solutions and business services that empower organizations and individuals through thoughtful design, innovative technology, and exceptional customer experiences.

## Vision

> To become one of Africa's most trusted technology companies, recognized globally for building world-class digital products, intelligent business solutions, and exceptional customer experiences.

## Purpose

> To help businesses and people solve complex challenges through intelligent systems, meaningful design, and technology that creates lasting value.

## North Star

> Unda Solutions exists to create intelligent solutions that improve how businesses and people work, connect, and grow. Through thoughtful design, innovative technology, and exceptional service, we build systems that inspire trust, create value, and stand the test of time.

## Brand promise

> We deliver thoughtful solutions that combine technology, design, and human-centered thinking to help businesses move forward with confidence.

## Core values

Craftsmanship, Innovation, Integrity, Simplicity, Impact, Collaboration, Continuous Learning, Customer Success. Full definitions and "what this means / what this doesn't mean" breakdowns are in the source Brand Foundation Manual — the short version: quality isn't optional, innovation is pursued with purpose not novelty, and customer success is the measure of the company's own success.

## Decision filter

Before pursuing any initiative, ask:

- Does this solve a meaningful problem?
- Does this create long-term value?
- Does this strengthen customer trust?
- Does this reflect our commitment to quality?
- Does this align with our core values?

If the honest answer to most of these is "no," reconsider the initiative regardless of how it was originally scoped.
