# Brand Voice & Tone

## Voice principles

The Unda Solutions voice is **Clear, Professional, Helpful, Honest, Confident, Human** — in that order of priority when they trade off against each other. Clarity wins over cleverness; honesty wins over sounding impressive.

## Tone by context

Tone flexes by context while the underlying voice stays constant:

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Documentation, guides, tutorials | Informative, patient, easy to follow |
| Business proposals, corporate comms | Confident, respectful, precise |
| Customer support, community | Warm, approachable, conversational (still professional) |
| Brand campaigns, milestones, launches | Optimistic, purposeful — not overly promotional |

## Writing style

- Plain language over unnecessary complexity.
- Concise — every sentence should carry meaning.
- Active voice ("We build scalable software solutions," not "Scalable software solutions are built by us").
- Explain technical concepts in accessible language for non-technical audiences; never assume prior knowledge.
- Structure with clear headings, short paragraphs, logical flow, bullet lists where appropriate.

## Messaging framework

Every piece of communication should answer, in order: **What is the problem? Why does it matter? How can we help? What is the outcome?**

## Vocabulary

**Preferred:** Solutions, Innovation, Technology, Design, Intelligence, Simplicity, Trust, Growth, Partnership, Quality, Systems, Experience, Reliability, Automation, Craftsmanship, Connected, Scalable, Modern, Human-Centered.

**Avoid:** Revolutionary, Disruptive, Game-changing, Best-in-class, World-leading (unless objectively supported), Magic, Effortless, One-click solution, Guaranteed success, Cutting-edge (unless contextually justified) — and corporate jargon like Synergy, Leverage, Pivot, Paradigm shift, Low-hanging fruit, Move the needle.

## Tone by division

Tone adapts slightly per division while the core voice stays recognizable:

- **Unda Studio** — slightly more expressive/creative, still professional.
- **Unda Technologies** — emphasizes expertise, precision, clarity.
- **Unda Travel** — welcoming, reassuring, service-oriented.

## Examples

**Good:** "We help organizations build scalable software solutions that simplify operations and create lasting value."

**Poor:** "We build revolutionary, game-changing software that will completely transform your business overnight."

**Good:** "Our team will guide you through each step of the visa application process."

**Poor:** "Don't worry — we'll magically handle everything."
