# 3. Art Direction

- **Photography** — real, specific, and unstaged-looking. People at work, real places, real product screens. No generic stock-library gloss, no isolated smiling-at-camera portraits.
- **Illustration** — used sparingly and only to explain a system (a diagram, a flow) — never as decorative filler for empty space. Geometric and flat; no mascots, no gradients-as-illustration.
- **Cropping** — tight and intentional; images are cropped to a single subject or moment rather than shown in full to "be safe."
- **Editorial approach** — treat imagery like a magazine treats reportage: one strong image says more than three mediocre ones. Full-bleed only when the image itself is strong enough to hold the entire frame.
- **Texture & background** — flat, quiet surfaces. Texture, when used, comes from real material (grain in a photo, weave in a product shot) — never from applied noise, gradients, or glassmorphism.

These constraints match and reinforce [`../design-system/foundations/photography.md`](../design-system/foundations/photography.md) and [`../design-system/foundations/illustration.md`](../design-system/foundations/illustration.md).
