# 9. Design Constraints

## Unda is

- Clear, restrained, structural
- Confident enough to leave space empty
- Consistent across every division
- Built for a decade, not a season

## Unda is not

- Playful for its own sake
- Loud, trendy, or maximalist
- A different brand per division
- Chasing the current design fashion

## Always avoid

- **Layout** — gradients as backgrounds, glassmorphism, skeuomorphism, decorative blobs, unnecessary illustration.
- **Typography** — more than two typefaces on one surface, decorative or script display fonts, more than 3–4 sizes on a single page.
- **Color** — saturated, high-chroma palettes; color used as the sole carrier of hierarchy or status.
- **Motion** — bounce/elastic easing, animation that runs longer than the user's patience, motion that exists only to "feel alive."

Any AI agent or designer proposing a pattern that falls in this list should stop and reconsider before shipping it — see [`../design-system/prompts/`](../design-system/prompts/index.md) and the design-system-level AI rules in `../CLAUDE.md` (Phase 7) once written.
