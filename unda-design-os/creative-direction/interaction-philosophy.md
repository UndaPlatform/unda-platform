# 7. Interaction Philosophy

- **Navigation** — flat and predictable; a user should always know where they are and how to get back, without a breadcrumb explaining it.
- **Buttons & feedback** — every action gets an immediate, visible response (state change, subtle motion, or message) — nothing is submitted into silence.
- **Hover states** — used to confirm interactivity, not to reveal new information a user needs to act.
- **Focus states** — always visible and never suppressed for aesthetics; keyboard and assistive-technology users get the same clarity as pointer users.
- **Information architecture** — shallow over deep; a structure a user can hold in their head beats one that is technically more "complete."
