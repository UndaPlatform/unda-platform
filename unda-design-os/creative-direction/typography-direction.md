# 5. Typography Direction

Typography is the primary visual identity of Unda Solutions — hierarchy is created through it before color, imagery, or ornament are considered. General Sans carries every brand and marketing moment (headlines, campaign pages, editorial writing at Unda Studio); Geist governs every product interface (dashboards, apps, forms) where legibility at small sizes and numeral clarity matter most; Geist Mono is reserved for data, code, timestamps, and technical labels.

- **Headlines** — large, confident, and set tight; a headline should be readable as a shape before it's read as words.
- **Paragraph rhythm** — generous line-height (1.6–1.75), a constrained measure, and a limited number of type sizes per page — hierarchy comes from spacing between blocks, not from adding new sizes.
- **Editorial layouts** (Unda Studio) — may introduce more expressive scale contrast between headline and body.
- **Product layouts** — smaller, denser type is acceptable, but line-height and contrast ratios are held to the same accessibility bar everywhere.

Literal type families, sizes, line-heights, and tracking values live in [`../design-system/tokens/typography-tokens.md`](../design-system/tokens/typography-tokens.md).
