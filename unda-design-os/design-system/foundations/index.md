# Foundations

The primitives everything else in this Design OS is built from. Where a topic has a companion "Tokens" file, this page defines the *system* (philosophy, structure, rules) and the Tokens page defines the *implementable values* (Phase 2 — not started yet).

**Status:** Complete for Phase 1.

## Contents

- [Color](./color.md) — brand color, division accents, neutral palette, semantic colors, usage ratio
- [Typography](./typography.md) — General Sans / Geist / Geist Mono, hierarchy levels, marketing vs. product usage
- [Spacing](./spacing.md) — 8pt grid, semantic scale, density
- [Grid & breakpoints](./grid-and-breakpoints.md) — 12-column grid, containers, responsive behavior
- [Radius & borders](./radius-and-borders.md) — shape language, radius scale, borders vs. shadows
- [Elevation & shadows](./elevation-and-shadows.md) — six elevation levels, surface hierarchy
- [Icons](./icons.md) — Lucide, rounded outline style, size scale
- [Illustration](./illustration.md) — minimal geometric style, composition rules
- [Photography](./photography.md) — editorial minimalism, composition, color treatment
- [Motion](./motion.md) — animation principles, categories, AI-specific motion
- [Accessibility](./accessibility.md) — WCAG 2.2 AA baseline, what it requires concretely
- [Dark mode & light mode](./dark-mode.md) — what stays the same, what changes

## Reading order for someone new

1. [Color](./color.md) and [Typography](./typography.md) first — the two biggest identity carriers.
2. [Spacing](./spacing.md) and [Grid & breakpoints](./grid-and-breakpoints.md) — the structural rules everything else sits inside.
3. [Radius & borders](./radius-and-borders.md) and [Elevation & shadows](./elevation-and-shadows.md) — the shape/depth language.
4. [Icons](./icons.md), [Illustration](./illustration.md), [Photography](./photography.md) — the supporting visual layers.
5. [Motion](./motion.md) and [Accessibility](./accessibility.md) — the interaction and inclusivity layer that applies across everything above.
6. [Dark mode & light mode](./dark-mode.md) last — it's a cross-cutting adjustment to everything already covered.
